What Family Orchards is growing

This page turns the farm plan into a simple customer-facing guide. It shows what is in production now, what is scheduled next, and when seasonal offerings are expected to reach retail sales.

Farm product catalog

Lemongrass

First commercial harvest targeted for 2026

  • 1 acre planted during 2024
  • Direct-to-consumer retail focus
  • Flagship early crop as harvest volume grows

Tilapia

Freshwater pond production beginning in 2026

  • Nile tilapia raised in an existing 10,000-square-foot pond
  • Projected annual stocking of 400–600 fingerlings
  • Targeted for direct retail sales after summer harvest

Sugarcane

Planting scheduled for March 2026

  • 4 acres planned for production
  • First harvest targeted for 2027
  • Part of the farm's projected revenue growth after 2026

Bamboo shoots

Planting scheduled for 2026

  • 0.5 acre dedicated to edible shoots
  • Harvest targeted roughly one year after planting
  • Focused only on food production, not pole sales

Christmas trees

First retail season targeted for 2030

  • 1.5 acres of pine trees planted during 2025
  • Long-term seasonal retail offering
  • Expected to become a major annual sales driver
